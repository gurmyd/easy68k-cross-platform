## Contract

## 1. Find reaches the document through the registry of core/documents

- Says: 3.9.1, "the editor component (E2) reaches it with `documentRegistryOf(ctx.documents)`".
  `DocumentService` has `text(id)` and `selection?(id)` but nothing that selects a range or replaces
  text, which Find and Replace must do.
- Does: `features/find/model.ts` (`registryTarget`) uses `documentRegistryOf(ctx.documents)` from
  `core/documents` (a feature may import `core/`): it reads the main selection of the document's
  `EditorState` and dispatches selection and change transactions there; the editor's views follow
  every transaction of the registry (E2's `DocumentEditor`). Replace All is one transaction (one
  undo step). With a document service that has no registry (the wave-0 stub) Find does nothing.
- Proposed for W1, if a contract form is wanted later: `DocumentService.select?(id, anchor, head)`
  and `DocumentService.replaceRanges?(id, changes, selection)`. Not needed now; no local adapter
  stands in for a missing member.

## Behaviour

## 2. The focus after a match (editor-ui §14.2 "the frame takes the focus")

- Says: found, the match is selected and the frame takes the focus (`Main->SetFocus()`).
- Found: `focusPanel(ctx, 'editor')` makes the shell call `focus()` on the editor's text element
  (`state/panels.ts` `focusPanelElement`), not CodeMirror's `view.focus()`. The browser then puts
  its DOM caret where the element last had it (or at its start, after a click on the dialog's
  button moved the page selection), and CodeMirror reads that back as a new selection: the match
  was selected and at once replaced by a caret at the start (seen in Chromium: Find button clicked,
  selection back at 0).
- Did, until gate I2: for 400 ms after a match the model watched the document in the registry and
  undid, once, the first selection change that came from the editor reading the browser's caret.
  The review of the package found that the watch also undid an arrow key pressed in that time
  (Find, Enter, ArrowRight left `[3,5]` selected instead of `[5,5]`); a first repair in this gate
  narrowed it to transactions without `scrollIntoView`.
- Does, since gate I2: nothing of its own. The cause is repaired where it lies: `state/panels.ts`
  (W3, errata W3.md entry 38) sends `PANEL_FOCUS_EVENT` to the element it is about to focus, and
  `ui/editor/DocumentEditor.tsx` (E2) answers it with `view.focus()`, which writes the state's
  selection into the page. `focusEditor` in `features/find/model.ts` now only calls
  `focusPanel(ctx, 'editor')`, and the watch is gone, so no keystroke of the student's is undone.
  Run with the watch switched off and those two changes in place, check-find.mjs passed in Chromium
  and WebKit; it now checks that the match is still selected once the editor has the focus after a
  click on the dialog's button (the case that failed before), and that ArrowRight and
  Shift+ArrowLeft right after a match act on it. find.test.ts checks that a selection change after
  a match is left alone.

## 3. Note 7 in detail

- Find without a selection starts at the caret. `End of document reached.` collapses the selection
  to its start; the next search starts from the top **while the text is unchanged and the caret is
  still where the box left it** (moving the caret or editing returns to "from the caret"). The
  original needed no such rule because it always started from the top without a selection.
- "Replace does not replace a selection that is not a match": such a selection counts as no
  selection with the caret at its start; the next match from there is found and, in the same press,
  replaced (as the original's step 1 finds and replaces in one press). A selection is a match when
  its text equals the Find text under Match case and, with Match whole word only, stands as a whole
  word.
- "An empty replacement does not skip a character": after an empty replacement the caret stays at
  the place of the deleted text and the next search starts there.
- Replace All: one undo step (note 5 lets the port use the editor's history).

## 4. Match whole word only: what a word character is

- Says: "the edit control's own definition of a word boundary" (§14.2), not specified further.
- Does: a match counts when the character before it and the character after it are not letters,
  digits or the underscore (Unicode letters, so `é` is a letter). UNVERIFIED against the RichEdit
  version the original runs on; the difference could only show for punctuation inside identifiers
  such as `.` or `@`, which both treat as boundaries here.
- Case-insensitive comparison folds each character (`é` = `É`), as Windows does for Windows-1252.

## 5. Small rules the documents leave open

- A selection with a line break, preloaded into the one-line Find box: its first line (an HTML text
  box cannot hold a line break). UNVERIFIED what the original's `TEdit` shows.
- Find... again while the dialog is open: the selection (if any) replaces the text and the focus
  goes back into the dialog (the button with text in the box, else the box). Find And Replace...
  again: both boxes take the selection (even empty) and the focus goes to the first box.
- Esc and the title-bar close button close the two dialogs (the original has neither; the kit's
  modeless Dialog has both). Listed in `docs/differences/E6.md`.
- The two dialogs float at the top right of the workspace (1.3.3, "docked at the top right of the
  editor area"); the one opened second goes under the first, so both stay usable.
- In Simulator and EASyBIN mode the editor's Find and Replace dialogs are not drawn; they stay open
  with their texts and come back in Editor mode (the original's editor and simulator were separate
  programs, so the editor's dialog never floated over the simulator).

## 6. Print: the range question (`print.range`), a dialog id not in 2.7

- Says: 1.4 note 2, printing goes through the system print dialog; editor-ui §13.1, the original's
  print dialog offers All / Selection (`Options = [poSelection]`). 2.7 lists no print dialog id.
- Found: a browser's (or the desktop app's) system print dialog cannot offer the editor's selection;
  it prints the page it is given.
- Does: while the active document has a selection, File > Print first opens a small modal dialog
  `print.range` (`Print`, group `Print range`, `&All` preselected as in the Windows dialog,
  `&Selection`, `OK`, `Cancel`); without a selection there is nothing to choose and the system
  dialog opens at once. Copies and the printer are chosen in the system dialog.
- Proposed text for 2.7: add `print.range` to the dialog ids, "(Print: All / Selection, asked only
  while the document has a selection)".

## 7. Print: what the print document keeps and what the browser decides

- Kept: the text is the selection or the whole document; `replaceTabs` exactly (`core/print/tabs.ts`:
  the column starts at 0 on every printed line, so a selection's first line counts from 0 although
  it may have begun mid-line; a tab worth 0 or less blanks is removed and moves the counted column
  back, `x += t`); the document's font and size (its own name first, then Courier New, Courier,
  monospace); black on white with no syntax colour (`color-scheme: light`, so the dark theme does
  not reach it); no header, line numbers or word wrap (over-long lines are clipped); a line that is
  exactly `NEW_PAGE_MARKER` is not printed and starts a new page; job title `EASy68K ` + the path,
  or the `untitledN.x68` name of a document without one (`Project.CurrentFile`).
- Page breaks follow the original's loop (`core/print/document.ts` `splitPages`): a marker on the
  first line gives an empty first page, two markers in a row an empty page between them, a marker on
  the last line no page after it; a text ending in a line break has no extra empty line (the edit
  control's line count); an empty text is one empty page.
- Decided by the browser, not reproducible: how many lines fit on a page (the original computes
  `PageHeight / TextHeight("W")`); hence also the original's empty page when a page fills exactly
  and the next line is a marker. The page margin is 12 mm (`@page`), where the original drew at 20
  device units from the printable area's edge; the browser may add its own header and footer, which
  its print dialog can switch off.
- Fixed tab size 0 with a tab character: the original divides by zero inside its print job and
  shows `Printer error.  Please check printer.`; the port shows the same box. Any rejection of
  `Platform.print` shows it too.

## 8. Printer Setup without a document

- Says: 1.4 note 2, "`Printer Setup...` opens the same dialog". The command is always enabled
  (editor-ui §2.1).
- Does: it hands the active document's whole text to `Platform.print` (the system dialog, where the
  printer is chosen; confirming prints it, as Print with All would); with no document open, an empty
  page. A web page has no other way to show the printer settings.

## 9. Defect seen while checking (for E3, with E2): the selection is not drawn

- Found: in the browser checks the match Find selects is never visibly highlighted, focused or not
  (screenshots `build/E6/find-check/*-found.png`, `*-end-of-document.png`); only the caret and the
  status bar show where it is. The selection state is right ([35, 39] in the 20.6 sequence).
- Cause, measured in Chromium: CodeMirror draws the selection in `.cm-selectionLayer` (z-index -2,
  behind the text), and `features/highlight/extension.ts:133` (E3) gives `.cm-content` an opaque
  background (`'&.e68-syntax .cm-scroller, &.e68-syntax .cm-content': { backgroundColor }`), which
  covers the layer. E2's `editor.css` has the right colours for the layer (focused and unfocused).
- Not worked around in E6 (not its files). Painting the background on `.cm-scroller` only, or
  making `.cm-content` transparent, would let the layer show.
- Gate I2: repaired by E3 (`features/highlight/extension.ts` now paints `.cm-scroller` and leaves
  `.cm-content` transparent). Find's match is drawn in the screenshots of check-find.mjs
  (`build/E6/find-check/*-found.png`).
