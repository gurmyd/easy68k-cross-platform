# Errata of I3 (release candidate)

Where `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (6.9, the I3 entry of 9.6) could not be followed as written or left a
choice, what the judges of `tests/integration/rc` do instead. No entry changes a contract.

## Contract

Nothing missing.

## 1. Item 8: "the automated scan" is the axe scan

- Says (6.9 item 8, with erratum X1 8): "the automated scan has no serious finding".
- Does: `item-08-a11y-lists.mjs` judges, in the logs of `check:a11y`, the checks named `axe ...`
  (the scan) and `a11y.spec.ts` in the e2e logs. Another check of the audit that fails (a keyboard
  walk, for example `walk-run` on the macOS runner in run 45034018006) is printed with its text and
  does not decide item 8; it makes its CI job red, which item 6 ("the CI matrix of 7.4 is green")
  counts.
- Why: the keyboard walks judge rules of 2.10 and 2.11 that a scan does not; counting them twice
  would hide which item a red job belongs to.

## 2. Item 6: the job `propose` of `oracle.yml` opens no pull request itself

- Says (7.4): `oracle.yml` "opens a pull request when a golden changed".
- Does: since erratum T1 27 the job pushes the candidate to the branch `oracle/regenerated-<run id>`
  and names in its summary the address at which a person opens the pull request, because the
  repository does not let GitHub Actions create pull requests. `item-06-platforms.mjs` accepts
  either form (`gh pr create`, or the branch and the `pull/new/` address).

## 3. One entry point and its logs

- Says (9.6 I3): "whatever makes the evidence re-runnable in `tests/integration/rc`".
- Does: `npm run check:rc` (`rc.mjs`) runs every local judge and prints one line per item;
  `fetch-ci.mjs` downloads, read-only, the job logs and job lists of the runs in `lib/runs.mjs`, and
  `check:rc -- --logs <folder>` judges them too. It is not part of CI: it exits 1 while an item does
  not hold, and the CI half of several items is the CI run itself.
- Since the reviews of the check it first runs the comparisons that items 2 and 5 read (the sim
  project of `test:conformance`, and the files the unit entries of the parity matrix name) and
  passes their Vitest JSON reports to the judges, so that a count of "identical" programs is what
  a run measured, not what a list leaves out. A judge that is not given its comparison exits 2
  (not shown), not 0.

## 4. Item 11: the word in lower case, and three statements of erratum E4

- Says (6.9 item 11): every item of the specs carrying the marker is either confirmed, or listed
  under "not yet confirmed" with the port's behaviour.
- Does: since the reviews of the check, the judge of item 11 searches `docs/porting` and its errata
  for the marker without regard to case. Of the 7 occurrences only in lower or mixed case, 4 are accounted
  for (carried by a list item, settled, or not the marker). Three are statements of erratum E4
  (the settings reader's `atoi` past 32 bits, Ctrl-Z as an end of file, the line break after the
  last line of `template.NEW`): they are errata of the port, not items of a spec, and nobody had
  yet said whether they are open items of item 11 (decided since, below). The judge prints them by name as awaiting E4's
  owner and does not count them against the item; any other lower-case occurrence fails it.
- Why: counting them would change a verdict on a question that is E4's owner's (and the owner of
  `docs/DIFFERENCES.md`, who would add their rows of part 4) to answer; ignoring them, as the
  upper-case search did, would hide them.
- Decided (the architect, 22 November 2026): the three are open verification items of item 11,
  because each says something about the original that nobody confirmed. Each is now a row of the
  table "The editor" in part 4 of `docs/DIFFERENCES.md` ("`atoi` in the settings files (erratum E4
  3)", "Ctrl-Z in the settings files (erratum E4 3)", "Save Template and the last line break
  (erratum E4 11)"), each statement in erratum E4 names its row, and the judge's `LOWER_CASE` table
  points the three to the list items `ed.E4.3a`, `ed.E4.3b` and `ed.E4.11`, which need those rows
  like every other open item: 0 await their owner, 86 list items, 59 open, each a row of part 4.
  Shown to fail: with a copy of `DIFFERENCES.md` without one of the three rows (each tried), "open
  and in NEITHER place: 1" naming that item, exit 1. The `awaiting` form stays in the judge for a
  statement whose owner has not yet decided; none uses it now. The verdict of item 11 does not
  change.

## 5. Item 10: the runner is read from the log

- Says (6.9 item 10): "on `ubuntu-latest`".
- Does: `item-10-stability.mjs` reads each log's "Image:" line (as `item-09-perf-verdict.mjs
  --require-ubuntu` does) and fails unless it names an Ubuntu image; `ubuntu-latest` itself is a
  label of the workflow that the log does not print (it prints the image, `ubuntu-26.04`).
